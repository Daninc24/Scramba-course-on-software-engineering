Top three activities to do at Nairobi

1. Visit the Nairobi National Park

Experience the unique wildlife and scenic landscapes of Nairobi National Park, located just outside the city center.

2. Explore the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust

Get up close with baby elephants and learn about conservation efforts at the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust.

3. Visit the National Museums of Kenya

Discover the rich cultural heritage and historical artifacts at the National Museums of Kenya.
